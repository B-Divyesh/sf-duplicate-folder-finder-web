Review before you remove.

Effective 27 August 2026

Mirrorbyte is a free utility provided under the MIT License. By using it, you agree to review its results and keep an independent backup before changing files.

What the result means

An “exact duplicate” means Mirrorbyte observed the same relative structure, file sizes, and SHA-256 content hashes at scan time. Unreadable files are excluded and called out as warnings. Files can change after a scan, so re-scan before cleanup when other programs may be writing to the folders.

Quarantine, not deletion

On supported Chromium browsers, Mirrorbyte can copy a selected duplicate into a timestamped quarantine folder, verify the copy, and then remove the original folder entry. It never empties quarantine. Browser, operating-system, permission, disk-space, and hardware failures remain possible.

Your responsibility

  • Confirm that the matching folder is a backup you intend to keep.
  • Check scan warnings and exported reports.
  • Maintain a separate backup before moving valuable data.
  • Restore or remove quarantined folders yourself.
