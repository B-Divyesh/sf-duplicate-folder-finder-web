Review before you remove.

Effective 28 August 2026

Mirrorbyte is free software under the MIT License. Review its results and keep a separate backup before changing files.

What an exact duplicate means

Mirrorbyte observed the same folder structure, file sizes, and content hashes at scan time. Warnings identify files it could not read.

Files can change after a scan. Scan again when another program may be changing the selected folders.

Move a copy into a holding folder

In Chrome or Edge, Mirrorbyte can move a reviewed copy into a dated holding folder. It verifies the copy before removing its original.

Move a held folder back to restore it. Mirrorbyte does not delete files from the holding folder.

Your responsibility

  • Confirm the matching folder is the copy you intend to keep.
  • Check scan warnings and exported reports.
  • Keep a separate backup before moving valuable data.
  • Restore or remove held folders yourself.
