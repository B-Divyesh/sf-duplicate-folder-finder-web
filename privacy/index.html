POLICY / LOCAL-FIRST

Privacy without fine print.

Effective 28 August 2026

Mirrorbyte processes folder names and contents inside your browser. It has no account, analytics, or tracking script.

Data on this device

The latest real report is stored in your browser. Demo reports use a separate store and are removed when you leave the demo.

The demo and legal pages reload offline after an online visit.

Server requests

The app does not send folder names, contents, or hashes to our server. Exports go only to the download location you choose.

Standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, requested URL, and time.

Delete local data

Clear this site’s browser data to remove reports, cached app files, and permissions. Manage holding folders through your operating system.

Contact

Open an issue in the public source repository. Do not include private file names or report contents.
